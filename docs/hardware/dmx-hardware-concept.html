A small, buffered DMX dongle

TI CC1352R1 radio + ESP32-S3 host + isolated DMX. A single supercapacitor bridges brief USB power interruptions.

USB-C + retention

Recessed 5 V input, status indicators, function button and a tether eye. Use a short, retained cable.

25 F internal buffer

One 2.7 V supercapacitor, charged to 2.6 V. About 26 s calculated at a 1.5 W load; verify on a prototype.

Plastic antenna tail
