# Bounded alternative-switch pass — no replacement selected

Two E-Switch families were checked, then sourcing stopped at the requested bound. PCM12 remains provisional; no new switch footprint, MPN, circuit or BOM substitution is claimed.

1. **EG1218**, official [current product/configurator](https://www.e-switch.com/product/eg-series-subminiature-slide-switch/) and its directly linked [P040040 RevC drawing](https://configured-product-images.s3.amazonaws.com/2D/specs/EG1218.pdf), read2026-10-22. Manufacturer currently lists/configures/orders this exact product; no formal lifecycle-guarantee field was shown. SPDT On-On,200mA30VDC gives ample normal termination current margin. However this is **top-actuated through-hole**, not the intended compact SMT side-access implementation. Body11.6×4×5.4mm plus2mm top actuator gives7.4mm nominal above PCB; untrimmed terminals extend4.7mm below. Recommended PCB holesØ.9 at2.5mm pitch are explicit, but body-to-hole origin is not fully dimensioned and no copper annulus is specified. It would require a changed access direction, lead trimming/underside clearance, assembly process and full body registration check. Do not treat the2mm actuator height alone as total switch height. This pass rejects it as a drop-in compact/access replacement rather than inventing a footprint/body datum.
2. **EG1315AA**, official [current product page](https://www.e-switch.com/product/eg1315-series-smt-right-angle-slide-switch/), read2026-10-22. This is a listed SMT right-angle product, but it is **SP3T rated25mA24VDC**, not SPDT. Its rating is below5V/120Ω=41.7mA nominal (and the existing6V conservative branch check is about51mA). Rejected at the electrical/circuit screen; no land extraction attempted. The higher voltage rating does not authorize a larger switched current at5V.

Source HTML/PDF and SHA256 values are retained in switch-alternative-receipt.json. No PCM lookup was repeated; no third family, purchase, supplier contact or authentication was attempted. Existing OFF-shipping latching setting, isolation separation, polymer access requirement and provisional PCM datum gate remain. These findings do not impose any new bus-surge requirement.

The separate requested placement timestamp correction was implemented and actually checked: raw netlist SHA changes after fresh KiCad exports are retained, while only top-level design/date text is ignored for acceptance.
