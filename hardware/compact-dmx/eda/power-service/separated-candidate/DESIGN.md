# Separated-power electrical prototype — not the active Rev C design

This is one complete, standalone two-sheet electrical candidate. Open [separated-candidate.kicad_sch](separated-candidate.kicad_sch), or read the [two-page PDF](separated-candidate.pdf). The [exported netlist](separated-candidate.net), [component ledger](component-ledger.csv), [pin/state results](validation.json) and [independent numerical proof](corner-proof.json) belong only to this directory. No active power contract, BOM, MCU/DMX capture, footprint or board was changed. The existing 500 mA normal-operation engineering basis and 25 mA nominal charging allocation are retained. Neither guaranteed compatibility with every 500 mA port nor a 1.5 A restriction is asserted.

The discrete control implementation is electrically reviewable but **does not fit the existing 25 × 6 mm power reservation**: 170 fitted components / 44 ICs, estimated body area **343.745 mm² excluding the cell**, 486.884 mm² including it, before pads, courtyards, routing, thermal copper or clearance. These are nominal-body and selected package-envelope estimates, not placement results. The existing AT25SF161B staging flash is included; MCU, DMX and external contacts are excluded. Two manual contacts are explicit fabricated normally-open PCB features. No new enclosure or cell has been selected. This candidate is not a compact-board or manufacturing release.

## Concrete power circuit

* U6 **TPS2553DBVR**, R_ILIM **61.9 kΩ**, powers LIMITED_5V directly from VBUS_RAW. Its value reserves current for the independent raw-AON feed. Main power passes through U19 **TPS2116DRLR** VIN1/manual mux to BACKUP_RAW and U4 **TPS62132RGTR**, fixed 3.3 V. U19 MODE is AON_2V5; PR1 is actual USB_VALID. No series Schottky is in the main path.
* U24 **TPS2553DBVR** has ILIM physically strapped to IN: the documented **50–100 mA DC** low-current mode. Its output CHG_IN powers U3 **BQ25173DSGR**. ISET is 100 Ω series then 11.9 kΩ to ground, with 100 nF on the resistor junction: 12.0 kΩ DC, nominal 25 mA. The datasheet recommends added ISET RC below 50 mA but does not specify these values; their stability is a prototype test gate. FB 22.5 kΩ/10.0 kΩ targets 2.6 V on the retained **HV1325-2R7156-R, 15 F / 2.7 V** cell.
* U5 **TPS61023DRLR** is fed exclusively by CAP_POS. XGL4020-222MEC is 2.2 µH ±20%; its nominal tolerance interval 1.76–2.64 µH lies within TI's effective-inductance recommendation. FB **562 kΩ/100 kΩ** targets 3.939 V PWM. BOOST_3V9 feeds mux VIN2. Its unknown maximum startup/precharge current is drawn from the cell rather than the USB main path. This removes the TPS61094 empty-cap output-precharge conflict; it does not create a specified maximum boost startup time or current.
* Raw AON is independent of LIMITED_5V: VBUS_RAW → **330 Ω** → BAT54H,115 → AON_RAW, diode-OR with BOOST_3V9 → second BAT54H,115 → AON_RAW. **TPS7A0225PDBVR** produces AON_2V5; **TPS3839G25DBZR** provides its reset. A 10 kΩ VBUS_RAW bleed provides a defined external-cycle discharge path. The independent raw feed lets the fault/control circuitry remain supplied while the limited main rail collapses. Diode full-temperature leakage/drop, LDO input-current limits and sub-1.65 V logic behavior remain physical gates.
* Buck magnetics retain **XFL4020-222MEC, 2.2 µH ±20%**. Buck SS is **100 nF**. Main reservoir is six **GRM21BR71A106KE51L, 10 µF / 10 V X7R** parts: 60 µF nominal, 33 µF minimum effective / 78 µF maximum allocated. Four more are on BACKUP_RAW and four on SYS_3V3; boost input/output each have two. CHG_IN has two **GRM155R61C105KA12D, 1 µF / 16 V X5R** parts. Local IC bypass and SS/ISET filters are **GRM155R71E104KE14D, 100 nF / 25 V X7R**. Effective capacitance and maximum tolerances used for transient estimates are allocations pending actual biased/temperature checks.
* Resistors use exact **Vishay TNPW e3 B/E** order codes: 0.1%, 25 ppm/K; 0402 through 100 kΩ, 0805 for 562 kΩ, 0603 for the raw-AON 330 Ω. The source/corner proof allows an additional ±0.25% temperature change over 100 K. Exact MPN, pins and endpoints are in the schematic and independent verifier.

## Hardware sequence and source truth

Four **TPS3700DDCR** dual monitors sense raw source, LIMITED_5V, common input, loaded cell, cell start, CHG_IN and boost output. Each open-drain monitor/fault signal has a 47 kΩ AON pullup and a real **SN74LVC2G17DCKR Schmitt buffer**; remaining gates use **SN74LVC2G08DCUR / SN74LVC2G32DCUR / SN74LVC2G04DCKR**. Four actual cross-coupled **SN74LVC2G02DCUR** SR latches store fault, cap arm, backup proven and charger arm. Every gate supply, unused input, converter enable and bypass capacitor is captured.

1. AON reset holds enables off; raw source qualification releases U6. With empty cap, the boost stays disabled. Actual LIMITED_5V qualification selects mux VIN1. Actual BACKUP_RAW readiness enables the buck independently of cell charge. DMX gates require buck enable and SYS reset release.
2. Only actual buck PG plus USB_VALID can set charger arm. The branch then precharges its own small input capacitors. BQ CE stays high until actual CHG_IN readiness. No timer forces source-good or hides a low-cap state. The cap and boost are absent from USB main startup energy demands, except for the bounded charger branch once enabled.
3. USB_VALID is the AND of **raw-source** and **LIMITED_5V** comparators. It never senses a cap-supported mux output as proof of source presence. Its 3.3 V status also requires SYS readiness. A source step outside these thresholds immediately removes the corresponding logical qualification after real propagation delay; there is no blanking period or commit-good override. Firmware commit policy remains unimplemented.
4. Cap arm requires USB_VALID, loaded-cap OK and the higher cell-start threshold. Boost must assert actual readiness before backup can authorize the buck; common-input readiness is also required. BACKUP_PROVEN records that both rails became ready. With source absent, loss of either proven rail or loaded-cap UVLO clears cap arm; rebound cannot rearm without external source. Backup startup at low loaded cell voltage is not assumed solely from enable assertion.
5. Persistent branch short need not keep the main load running. Crucially, **charger arm survives buck-PG / LIMITED_5V-qualifier loss while raw input remains valid**. This lets the real TPS2553 5–10 ms fault deglitch complete instead of repeatedly disabling the branch before its fault can assert. Main and branch FAULT are wired-OR into the raw-AON fault latch. A latched fault removes main/buck/charger/boost enables; fault clearing on disabled switches cannot restart them. External raw disappearance (about 0.8 V detector) or AON reset clears the latch. A sustained rated raw source is required to verify this physical sequence; upstream source collapse is outside the static latch proof.

Detector corners including resistor tolerance/temperature and input-bias allowance are recorded for every edge in corner-proof.json. USB rising range is 4.055–4.194 V; common/boost-ready rising range 3.563–3.685 V; loaded cap falling range 1.631–1.706 V; cell-start rising range 2.176–2.248 V. These separated thresholds establish DC ordering. Comparator propagation, SR hazards, switching delays and subminimum-supply behavior are not established by Boolean enumeration.

## Independent current, startup and energy assessment

The proof reads the exported netlist, without importing the generator or active budget. TPS2553 DC equations with the selected resistor give **380.205 mA minimum / 477.081 mA maximum** main limit. Adding maximum main quiescent current, raw-AON resistor short current and raw monitor/bleed currents gives a **494.829 mA component DC envelope** under 5.5 V input. The margin below 500 mA is only **5.171 mA**. This is not a hot-plug/overshoot bound or USB compliance result.

| State / allocation | Main current | Margin to 380.205 mA minimum |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Healthy, 4.4 V source and allocated switch/mux resistance | 330.907 mA | 49.298 mA |
| Healthy, lowest qualified source minus 50 mV step allowance | 363.638 mA | 16.567 mA |
| Branch regulated at its 100 mA fault maximum, normal source | 402.611 mA | −22.406 mA |
| Same branch fault at lowest qualified allocation | 435.341 mA | −55.136 mA |

Healthy figures use **1.1 W SYS / 85% buck efficiency**, 2 mA auxiliary allocation, KISET-based upper charging estimate and branch overhead. Radio/DMX/flash/boot currents are not measured; CC1352R shares 2.4 GHz and US915, with no independent simultaneous-radio assumption. A branch fault can exceed the minimum main limit: safe latched shutdown is the selected response, not continued operation. If the branch switch itself fails short, the upstream main limiter/fault path remains the protection path; transistor failure modes, overshoot, SOA and thermal response require physical validation.

For 2.6 µF maximum allocated CHG_IN capacitance, a 100 mA branch edge and available minimum main-current headroom, the deficit-charge estimate is 3.204 µC. The 33 µF effective main reservoir yields 97.1 mV droop and **36.0 mV remaining USB falling-threshold margin after a further 50 mV allowance**, at the actual LIMITED_5V sense node. Six reservoir capacitors were selected because four failed this calculation. Initial total main capacitance is allocated at 130 µF maximum; ideal constant-current fill scale is 1.881 ms. Branch scale at 50 mA is 0.286 ms. Both are below the 5 ms minimum switch fault deglitch in the ideal model. This is **not** a specified maximum startup time: real converter delays, effective C, leakage, nonlinear load, source/cable response and limit overshoot remain open.

Buck SS minimum-capacitance / 2.7 µA maximum-current arithmetic gives a 35.42 ms ramp allocation, with 4.85 mA maximum allocated output-cap charging current. It does not bound MCU/DMX boot load or mux softstart. Buck EN/SS have VIN+0.3 V absolute maxima: enabled steady-state levels fit below the lowest qualified common input, but fast input collapse versus gate propagation and SS discharge must be checked physically. Raw-AON static inventory is checked against a 2 mA allocation; with 0.7 V allocated diode drop its input floor is 3.038 V at 4.4 V source. The 0.7 V full-temperature drop and LDO ground current are assumptions, not newly invented datasheet guarantees.

BQ's nominal 25 mA implies **26 minutes ideal 15 F to 2.6 V**; capacitance/current corners give approximately **38.4 minutes before CV/thermal effects** (see exact numeric receipt). KISET limits are specified under higher output-voltage test conditions, not across 0–2.65 V; low-cell current accuracy and missing FB input-bias bound remain open. The separate 50–100 mA branch limits healthy/fault input demand in DC even when charging accuracy is unknown. Cold-cap normal linear dissipation is approximately 0.152 W; a shorted branch limiter can initially dissipate about 0.55 W and the main limiter more. These are power estimates, not a thermally qualified layout.

Runtime keeps the earlier conservative **2.448 V** start allocation, 9.45 F effective cell, 80% combined efficiency, 1.11 W load, and earliest 1.706 V loaded cutoff: **10.497 s**, only **0.497 s** margin over 10 s. It accounts for the prior hysteresis reserve rather than silently reclaiming it for continuous-CV charging. Cell ESR, age, actual loaded cutoff, boost current capability and real RF/DMX load can consume that margin. No guaranteed 10–30 s runtime is claimed. Eaton specifies the retained cell to +65 °C; a broader assembly temperature rating is not inferred from IC ratings.

## Boundary, sourcing and verification limits

Existing MCU18 / DMX7 / factory6 contact maps and power/status names are preserved. USB data contacts are explicit NC; passive Rd is retained. USB-C shell/ESD, barrel connector/mechanical adapter and source compliance are parent integration gates. SERVICE remains separate from ROM. INPUT_FAULT_N remains a local 2.5 V pullup: **no direct MCU connection and no implemented power-off-safe fault translator**. A future translator needs parent coordination; DIO18 is not allocated here. Staging SPI flash is retained, including EP-to-ground recommendation; WP is register-controlled protection, not a complete power-fail commit policy. No resident loader or firmware was written.

Actual KiCad 10.0.7 CLI ERC reports **0 errors / 0 warnings / 0 exclusions**, using default rules. The sole supported pin-mode specialization is U24 ILIM: TI labels this analogue programming pin O but explicitly permits ILIM=IN. The mode-specific symbol models that strap terminal as passive analogue configuration; the verifier requires the hard IN tie and applies the 50–100 mA bound. U6's resistor-programmed ILIM remains an output. There are no phantom internal source boundaries or flags on unfinished control nets: the four flags identify actual external supply/return, physical diode-OR output and buck-inductor output.

The netlist exporter emits an **annotation warning** because this prototype retains descriptive symbolic references such as R_ILIM and C_LIMIT1. The independent verifier requires unique references and every expected pin/passive; no missing or duplicate component is accepted. Conventional final annotation and footprint mapping are still needed before layout/BOM integration. Footprints are deliberately not assigned in this owned electrical candidate.

verify.py independently checks literal MPN/pin maps, passive MPN coding/value/ranges, all actual gate pins, 36,864 stable sensor/prior-state combinations and temporal empty-cap/fault/backup/no-rearm scenarios. This establishes static circuit/logic consistency, not arbitrary analogue timing. corner-proof.py checks 256 detector corners, source/buck headroom, charge-resistor arithmetic, AON DC inventory, Schmitt levels, minimum-limit load margins, input-reservoir allocation and conditional energy. PDF/SVG were visually inspected for page clipping/overlap. Sources and cache hashes are in source-manifest.json; web-only references and failed full-PDF fetches are distinguished from cached originals.

Before any manufacturing acceptance, perform the physical checks enumerated in corner-proof.json: single-attach empty/partial/full cell; brownout and external-cycle recovery; persistent branch failure; all enables/PG/AON/source traces; boost startup/low-VIN/PFM/current/ESR/switchover; BQ low-cell accuracy/filter/CV/thermal; biased capacitance; loaded runtime; radio/DMX/flash boot peaks; raw-AON/leakage/SR transitions; USB inrush/ESD/compliance; and actual placement/routing/thermal fit. In particular TPS61023 has no stated PFM maximum FB voltage or maximum startup current/time; TPS2116 startup timing is typical only. These remain precise source/bench gates, not reasons to leave the circuit uncaptured.

## Reproduce locally

From this directory, using the already installed KiCad Flatpak:

```sh
python3 generate.py
flatpak run --command=kicad-cli org.kicad.KiCad sch export netlist --format kicadxml -o separated-candidate.net separated-candidate.kicad_sch
flatpak run --command=kicad-cli org.kicad.KiCad sch erc --format json --severity-all --exit-code-violations -o erc.json separated-candidate.kicad_sch
flatpak run --command=kicad-cli org.kicad.KiCad sch export pdf -o separated-candidate.pdf separated-candidate.kicad_sch
python3 verify.py
python3 corner-proof.py
```
