### Electrical obligations before any12V capture

1. **Rework input qualification/protection.** Existing TPS259474 UVLO≈4.1–4.3V/OVLO≈5.6–5.8V rejects12V. Choose bounded healthy-source thresholds, fault cutoff, hysteresis/restart behavior and current protection for the new load/voltage. Its23V recommended input ceiling is not universal fault survival. Recheck all divider pin voltages, EN paths, power dissipation, dVdt voltage requirement(VIN+5V), timing/inrush and latch reset. Reverse blocking must still prevent converter/USB backdrive into the isolated module; unpublished response/leakage maxima remain open.
2. **Requalify the regulator and every support part.** TPS63070 recommended VIN max16V, absolute max20V. IRM20-12 lists OVP13.8–16.2V: its upper number already exceeds16V operation. RAC20 lists150–195% OVP, up to23.4V at12V, above20V absolute maximum. TRACO's120% OVP is typical, with no guaranteed fault ceiling. None is a sufficient input clamp by itself. Existing C1/C2 GRM21BC71E106ME11L, C3 TMK107BBJ106MA-T, C13 TMK105B7104MV-FR and C12 GRM155R71E103KA01D must be checked by exact rated voltage, effective capacitance at new bias, ripple/ESR and fault voltage; no blanket claim that the bank is10V or that it automatically works at12V. Re-evaluate XFL4020-152MEC inductance/saturation/hot losses and the full buck filter/loop/layout. The normal duty ratio is above the published30% minimum, but fault/OVP corners are a separate gate. The2A output maximum leaves only89mA above the1.911A model; that number is not guaranteed thermal headroom. TI's3.05A input-limit minimum is tested at VIN5V/VOUT6.5V, not a transferable12V buck guarantee.
3. **Keep12V away from USB.** TPS25810 remains supplied by regulated5V only: with IN1/IN2/AUX tied together, their common recommended range is4.5–5.5V(IN1 alone permits6.5V); the pin absolute maximum is7V. Input/output pass-through faults, converter switch faults and feedback faults need an independent5V output overvoltage interruption/barrier with response and energy qualification. PG is neither precision USB-valid qualification nor that barrier. Existing ESD suppression is not DC OVP. Maintain CHG/CHG_HI fixed1.5A, both CC orientations, VCONN limits, cold-socket discharge, passive D+/D− short, and no PD escalation. The original410mA VCONN stress is a current-limit maximum, not a healthy operating-current entitlement(the recommended VCONN source current is250mA). The controller's upper1.82A VBUS current-limit corner plus410mA VCONN and1mA allocation totals2.231A, beyond the regulator's2A output rating: a fault-limited state is a separate protection/thermal test, not promised continued operation. The old≈3A input breaker cannot be assumed to protect every12V-buck output overload.
4. **Preserve isolation boundaries.** Module primary/secondary insulation remains stationary. Only the two isolated LV conductors cross the rotary joint; return is shared among module secondary, regulator and USB.12V introduces no extra galvanic barrier. Mains/PE never crosses the joint; connector shell/accessible metal policy and each PowerCON/TRUE1/NEMA variant need independent protective construction. Requalify FPC voltage rating, terminations, strain relief and fault energy. Healthy DC current alone proves neither wire protection nor startup. Module hiccup, eFuse timing, constant-power collapse, output-bank/USB inrush, unload overshoot, rebound and hotplug must be bounded or measured.

The69.9/73.1mm cylindrical examples may be less compact than a rectangular fixed pod and do not dictate the final shape. Exact pod location, endcap overlap, enclosure clearances and assembly access require one subsequent allocation CAD pass. Prefer the cached **IRM-20-12** voltage variant for that electrical prototype only after the listed12V protection/regulation obligations are captured and checked. If a coaxial all-in-one body is retained instead, redo its entire module/trunk/endcap allocation against the published spacing; do not resume R1 jig development in the blocked43mm bore.
