# Axial routed rotating source prototype

This independent branch repacks and routes the existing regulated-source circuit with the USB port directed along **+X**. The supported PCB is **27 × 20 × 1 mm**, four copper layers, all components on top. The 25 × 18 mm target is **not achieved**. The actual connector, assembly and FPC reserves require parent/Astra integration; this is not an accepted collar fit or manufacturing release.

Editable files: [schematic](regulated-source.kicad_sch), [real exported netlist](regulated-source.net), [routed board](source-axial.kicad_pcb), [schematic PDF](regulated-source.pdf), [copper layers PDF](copper.pdf), [assembly PDF](assembly.pdf), [native layer SVGs](svg), [package allocations](source-axial-envelopes.step), [editable envelope CAD](source-axial-envelopes.FCStd), [assembly reserves](assembly-reserves.step). The STEP solids are package/slot allocations, not detailed vendor connector models.

The circuit remains stationary IRM-20-5 → existing two-conductor FPC → TPS259474LRPWR → TPS63070RNMR forced-PWM 5.136 V → TPS25810RVCR fixed 1.5 A advertisement → USB4105-GF-A. No mains, PE, endcap, core, sidecar, cell-protection, firmware or active BOM changes are made. All original parts, values and external net boundaries remain. Two source-backed passive additions are explicit:

* **R12 TNPW040210K0BYED, 10 kΩ:** PROTECTED_IN → REG_EN_SERIES → U2.14. TPS63070 §11.1 requires a 10 kΩ series resistor when EN is tied to VIN. This is an internal net split; it adds no supply or control architecture.
* **R13 TNPW0402100RBYED, 100 Ω:** U1.7/INPUT_DVDT → R13 → INPUT_DVDT_C/C12.1 → C12 → isolated ground. TPS25947 §7.3.5.1, printed p30, recommends 100 Ω in series when CdVdt exceeds 10 nF. C12 remains GRM155R71E103KA01D, 10 nF ±10%, so its initial upper value is 11 nF. Vishay 28758 ordering tables support 0402, 100R, B ±0.1%, Y ±10 ppm/K, ED packaging. The nominal series RC is 1 µs; its tolerance-only 0.9–1.1 µs scale is not a converter startup or fault-response bound.

The source GLM review found no new demonstrated electrical defects. Its TPS25810 falling-threshold interpretation was corrected against the primary p6: 0.65 V is minimum, 0.875 V typical. This candidate checks the **recommended operating** EN limits from §6.3: PG maximum low 0.4 V < **0.63 V**, and divider/leakage minimum high ≈2.491 V > **1.17 V**. PG is a converter-ready control, not an externally qualified 4.75 V status. No source-good output or future install permission is inferred from it.

## Routing and source guidance

Sources are the exact fourteen cached primary PDFs in [sources.json](sources.json); no new family or vendor land pattern is substituted. The source references below refer to those PDFs and their cached text, not search snippets. The original reviewed capture stays untouched except the parent's separately documented doc/check/inventory changes.

TPS63070 SLVSC58B §11.1/§11.2 calls for short/wide main paths, close input/output capacitors, and quiet control ground joining power ground near the IC. L1 is adjacent to U2; L1/L2 switching paths stay entirely on top, with 0.22 mm package escapes widening to 0.8 mm. Close C3 and C7 are 0603 ceramics; their power nodes enter paired VIN/VOUT vias. Feedback has a dedicated top Kelvin connection from **C7.1 to R6.1**, with U2.5 to the R6/R7 junction; it does not sense at the USB switch or bulk capacitor. VAUX has only its local C11. PS/SYNC and VSEL stay low, independent of software.

U2 control-ground/C11/R7 returns are connected on their own top/In2 tree. An actual In1 copper void prevents their through-vias joining the power plane early. They join at the **(9.35, 8.70) mm** local star. U2 PGND uses a separate short top stripe with two power-ground vias. The input and output buses use **0.9 mm B.Cu**, with only short package/connector necks narrower. In2 carries low-current control paths, not the main current. In1 is the power-ground plane except for the explicit control-ground void.

TPS25947 SLVSFC9C §8.4 asks for close ≥0.1 µF input bypass, wide current paths, local quiet ground and thermal spreading vias. C13 is directly connected to the U1 IN via by a short top trace; its separate power-ground via at (3.5,8.55) mm connects through an explicit plane tongue. U1 divider/ILM/DVDT returns have a separate top/In2 tree with a local star at **(2.00, 13.50) mm**; a deliberate B.Cu strap joins that star to the dedicated power-ground via at **(3.40, 14.60) mm**. The checker separately proves that this strap touches only those two vias and that other quiet vias do not bypass the star through the plane. U1's two IN and two OUT off-device vias spread current/heat; this is not the eight-under-device JEDEC thermal simulation pattern or a reproduction of the published θJA board.

TPS25810 SLVSCR1C §10.1 asks for ≥120 µF near the input, ≥10 µF near the port, close 100 nF bypass, short REF/REF_RTN, short CC paths and multiple exposed-pad ground vias. C8 supplies the same 150 µF nominal polymer/120 µF minimum at the 25°C tolerance reference; its electrical/thermal validation remains open. C10 is next to the IN/AUX pins and C9 near the port. REF and REF_RTN run on top directly to opposite ends of R10; **REF_RTN is never ground**. CC1 and CC2 remain distinct and independently checked. The four actual D+/D− contacts are shorted directly by top copper, with no processor/data communication. The saved path resistance screen uses hot copper, not a claim of USB certification.

U3 has **six 0.30 mm finished-drill / 0.60 mm pad through-vias** in its EP, on 0.6 × 0.8 mm pitch, plus two input and two output current vias. TI specifies multiple EP vias and calls for filled/plugged/tented under-paste vias; six and the chosen pitch/drill are this engineering allocation. They are not a TI mandated count, not guaranteed thermal performance, and require a supplier-approved via-in-pad/finish/stencil process. Bulk/FPC lands have four current/return vias each. The actual complete via geometry is in route-ledger.json and physical-geometry.json.

The proposed stack is 70 µm F.Cu / 100 µm dielectric / 35 µm In1 / 570 µm core / 35 µm In2 / 100 µm dielectric / 70 µm B.Cu, with 10 µm mask each side: nominal 1.00 mm. FR4 εr4.2/loss0.02 are placeholders for supplier qualification. Outer-copper minimum 63 µm is an engineering screen, not a purchased fabrication guarantee. A legacy IPC-2221 external-trace calculation (k=.048, ΔT40 K, width0.9 mm, copper63 µm) gives about6.6 A for an isolated uniform trace; it is only a preliminary engineering screen for the main bus. It does not qualify the 0.22 mm short package escapes, paired via current sharing, plane return, inner controls, solder joints, board/enclosure temperature, or transient I²t. No measured temperature rise or IPC-certified ampacity is claimed. Native design rules remain 0.20 mm clearance/track minimum, 0.30 mm minimum drill, 0.25 mm hole-to-copper, 0.50 mm copper-to-edge. No DRC exclusions or global rule reductions are used.

## Exact local mechanical frame

The PCB spans **X0..27, Y0..20, Z−1..0 mm**. X is axial, +X toward the USB mouth; Y is tangential, and Z is the component normal. Parent/Astra owns the final barrel transform. The port reference is the PCB right-edge point **(27,10,0) mm**, mouth direction **(+1,0,0)**, with the actual solder row at **X22.245 mm**. This resolves the old Y-edge orientation; rotating the old board is not the implementation.

The unchanged primary GCT lands have twelve signal lands for sixteen contacts, four plated shell slots and two NPTH peg holes. Their actual holes are cut in the STEP PCB. The conservative derived connector body allocation spans X21.52..29.17, Y5.455..14.545, Z0..3.46 mm. The PCB edge is the exact integration datum; supplier mating-face/profile alignment relative to it still needs confirmation against the detailed mechanical connector. Do not use the allocation front face as an invented exact mating plane. Body maximum **3.46 mm** exceeds the old3.2 mm target by0.26 mm. With nominal1 mm PCB, body-to-PCB-bottom is4.46 mm; proposed PCB0.9..1.1 mm and sourced stake length0.8..1.1 mm give a conservative4.56 mm normal envelope, excluding assembly clearance. Up to0.2 mm stake projection under a thin board needs retention/fillet approval. Stake STEP solids are the swept slot allocations, not invented metal cross-sections.

No bottom components are used. Top assembly clearance is reserved to Z3.71 mm and bottom clearance to Z−1.35 mm. The 20 mm FPC keeps its original full solder-land access: X0.225..20.775, Y17.4..20, with solder-tool space Z0.3..3.46. A separate **external clamp/strain-relief reserve** X0.225..20.775, Y20..24, Z0..2 is shown; a clamp is not implemented or qualified. Ribbon coverlay, lead-out direction, solder creep, flex cycle/bend and cable strain tests remain open.

This one routed topology needs **27 ×20 mm PCB**, 20% more area than25×18, plus connector overhang, assembly clearances and the external FPC clamp. All32 purchased parts plus the two fabricated land/probe footprints are present:34 footprints,127 numbered pad primitives/129 including NPTH. Their complete courtyard sum is354.580 mm² and physical body/land projection sum220.0685 mm²; neither sum proves packing. Minimum courtyard gap is0.110 mm, with no supplier land shrinking. The parts and route reservations did not support the25×18 allocation in this bounded pass; this is not a proof that no other layout can ever fit. Do not enlarge the OD/core or accept a collar fit silently. The correct result for integration is a larger routed source allocation requiring explicit physical disposition.
