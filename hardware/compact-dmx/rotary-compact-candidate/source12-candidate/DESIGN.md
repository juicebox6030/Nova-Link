# S12-R1: isolated 12 V bus to independently disconnected 5 V USB

This is one captured, **unrouted development prototype**, with a conditional healthy DC pass. It is **not qualified protection or a manufacturing release**. The static output barrier interrupts a sustained buck-to-input fault, but the available primary specifications do not bound the injected energy sufficiently to guarantee USB stays below 5.5 V, or even prove pin survival during every fault edge. That is the decisive unresolved protection gate. Stop at this captured candidate; no further architecture/family search is included.

All writes belong to this directory. Existing source boards, sourcepod studies, cell protection, sidecar, canonical BOM/CAD and all baseline contracts remain unchanged by this task. Never apply 12 V to AXIAL-R1. IRM-20-12 is only a development source allocation; its installation, stationary pod, mains/PE/endcaps and sealed thermal approval remain external gates. `protected-inputs.json` retains the initial 1,123-file snapshot. `observed-external-deltas.json` identifies the one concurrent parent-owned README change; it is not accepted or silently refreshed. Electrical, numerical, source, footprint and mechanical protected inputs still match.

## Actual captured circuit

`J0 → U1 → J2 → external FPC → J3 → U2/L1 → U4 → U3 → J1`.

* **Stationary U1 TPS259474LRPWR** is before the FPC, unlike the old rotating-side breaker. It provides true reverse blocking, adjustable input UVLO/OVLO, startup slew control and latched circuit-breaker shutdown. R5 = 2.00 kΩ; ITIMER is deliberately open for the published fastest breaker mode. R6 = 100 Ω is in series with C11 = 10 nF: its +10% corner exceeds TI's 10 nF boundary (§7.3.5.1). Breaker action is delayed, not an instantaneous current ceiling or an FPC fuse/I²t proof. An input power cycle/appropriate EN reset is required after a latched fault.
* **Rotating U2 TPS62135RGXR**, 4 A, MODE tied VIN for forced PWM, VSEL tied GND, R7/R8 = 64.0 kΩ/10.0 kΩ, produces 5.18 V nominal. VOS senses the positive output-capacitor node, separate from the divider. SS/TR has C10 = 10 nF. L1 = XAL4020-152MEC, 1.5 µH. FB2 is genuinely unused. It has **11 physical pads including actual VIN/SW/GND bars; there is no invented twelfth exposed pad**.
* **U5 TPS3700DDCR**, powered independently from protected 12 V, senses the **pre-disconnector** buck rail through R11/R12 = 100 kΩ/9.09 kΩ (UV) and R13/R14 = 100 kΩ/8.00 kΩ (OV). OUTA and OUTB are open-drain, wire-AND to U4 EN, with R15/R16 = 100 kΩ/100 kΩ. A persistent high buck output therefore holds U4 off, even after SAFE5 discharges. U5 is not powered from its own switched output.
* **U4 TPS25940LRVCR** interrupts between buck and TPS25810, and provides true reverse blocking. Its own OVP (R17/R18 = 100 kΩ/20 kΩ) is an independently implemented, higher backstop, including while U5 is starting. R19 = 24.9 kΩ sets its current threshold; DEVSLP is grounded. C12+C13 = 20 nF nominal controls output slew. **Only thermal shutdown is latched in this L variant. OVP/UVLO recover automatically**; do not describe every fault as latched. External power cycling is acceptable if comparator hysteresis prevents recovery to a normal high-corner rail.
* **U3 TPS25810RVCR** receives only SAFE5 on all IN1/IN2/AUX pins. CHG high, CHG_HI low selects fixed **1.5 A** CC advertisement, no higher voltage PD and no data processing. U2 PG and U4 PGOOD gate U3 EN as open-drain outputs; they are sequencing aids, **not a precision source-valid signal or future install permission**. There is no exported qualified source-good status. R24 is 100 kΩ between REF and REF_RTN, not ground. Both CC orientations and the manufacturer's powered detach/discharge behavior are retained.
* **J1 USB4105-GF-A** uses the real 16 contacts on twelve shared signal lands, four shell stakes and two NPTH holes. All four D contacts share a copper-short net (a future routed D+/D− <200 Ω requirement, **not yet a measured/routed path**). SBU pads are physically present and NC. Shell-to-isolated-GND is a proposal; no rotating PE and no claim of approved shell/EMC policy. D1 TPD1E10B06DPYR is ESD protection, **not a DC OVP clamp**.

J0/J2/J3 are fabricated solder lands, not fictitious purchased connectors. Their wire, coverlay, clamp, strain relief, shielding and assembly processes need qualification. TP1/TP2 expose only diagnostic fault nodes; no firmware or MCU boundary is introduced.

## Primary sources and regulator selection

Two regulator choices were inspected, exactly one captured. TPS62130 (SLVSAG7F, §6.5) has wider PFM regulation bounds and automatic PFM/PWM, making the small USB/OVP window less convenient. ## Source/current/fault and energy limits

IRM-20-12 nominal12 V/1.8 A; source tolerance±2.5% already includes line/load. The0..50 °C coefficient allowance is±0.03%/K from25 °C. Reserving the entire200 mVpp ripple on each unfavorable side gives an **11.41–12.59 V instantaneous engineering screen**, not an invented±100 mV symmetric ripple waveform.16.2 V is the module OVP endpoint, not a specified transient waveform/ceiling under every failure. At16.2 V, U1 is statically off; U2's17 V operating input, U4/U5's18 V operating limits and25 V input capacitors retain DC room while fault interruption occurs. Cable surge/overshoot beyond these values is unqualified. Source study remains limited to the datasheet's full-power line/temperature region (planning115 VAC or higher,0..45 °C); no sealed pod/collar thermal grant follows from free-air curves.

The finite path uses1.046167656 m, finished7.8 mm width,70 µm copper−10%,80 °C copper: flex loop **92.0836 mΩ**. Add20 mΩ terminations,45 mΩ U1, allocated actual hot fanout16.25025 mΩ and stationary wire4.96437 mΩ: **178.29823 mΩ** total. These inherited resistances are modeling allocations, not production-cable measurements. U1 is stationary; its R belongs in input-power iteration before/through the flex. The two J2/J3 land pairs share net names but the external resistance is never idealized away in the budget.

The output loads are1.5+.410+.010 = **1.920 A** stress and1.82+.410+.010 = **2.240 A** USB-limit fault stress.10 mA output auxiliary and2 mA input auxiliary are conservative allocations, not measured full-device maxima.12 corners iterate constant input power with80/85/90% assumed efficiency and both source bounds. **No efficiency minimum is obtained from typical graphs.** Worst2.240 A/80% case gives **1.318556 A FPC**,11.174904 V at buck,0.160096 W flex copper loss,0.309988 W whole-path loss and2.942478 W allocated converter loss. Old5 V source/breaker failure is not carried forward as a pass on the old board.

For the1.920 A stress case alone, the same worst assumptions give1.127039 A FPC,370 mA breaker-floor margin,0.116966 W flex loss and2.522124 W allocated buck loss. These loss assumptions expose the thermal burden rather than establishing that the small package/collar can dissipate it. The conditional SAFE5 healthy minimum is4.933 V, above TPS25810's4.5 V recommendation. A separate all-pin operating-limit screen is saved in `electrical-check.json`.

U2's published positive switch current minimum4.8 A exceeds a **conditional**2.956 A inductor peak screen using1.8 MHz (engineering frequency assumption), initialL−20% plus10% bias loss, not a guaranteed min-frequency/bias corner. Published switching frequency is typical; actual high/low frequency, hot saturation and current response need checking. A4 A output rating alone does not prove delivery at every temperature/layout. At1.920 A, U4 worst DC conduction alone is0.236 W; U3 VBUS1.5 A worst conduction is0.124 W, plus VCONN/device heat. Copper and thermal work is still absent.

U4 at R19=24.9 kΩ has **two different published tests**: overload3.25–3.81 A at1 V drop; short3.21–3.77 A atVIN12 V/5 V drop, each widened by external resistor tolerance. Neither is an instantaneous injected-current limit. Sustaining full U4-limit constant power can exceed U1's breaker floor; safe input latch-off is an acceptable response. There is no requirement to continue operating through a failed branch.

**Fault counterexample:** at the latest static precision trip5.476745 V, a120 µF minimum (25 °C tolerance only) polymer has only **2.7905 µC** headroom to5.5 V. Even assuming current already limited to3.8176 A, that is only **0.731 µs**. An illustrative20 µs injects0.636 V; it is not a bound. U5 gives18/29 µs typical propagation at5 V/10 mV overdrive, not a maximum at this12 V bias. U4's2 µs typical OVP delay is to FLT under its specified overdrive, not guaranteed final channel isolation. The backup can trip as late as6.09 V. Therefore the capture establishes independent **static** interruption of a persistent buck input/switch/feedback fault, **not acceptable maximum USB surge or7 V pin survival**. A single shorted U4 would defeat its interruption; this is not an all-single-fault safety claim. No comparator PG flag or ESD diode repairs that missing energy bound.
