# Compact interface repair — mechanical prototype

This candidate changes only this directory. The frozen electrical boards are copied byte for byte; they remain **unrouted and electrically unqualified**. Mechanical validation does not establish source protection, USB compliance, thermal capacity, dynamic-flex life, mains construction or a released product envelope. The parent repair brief is a parent engineering instruction, not an owner-verbatim correction.

The primary branch repair uses explicit swept Ø3 mm conductor envelopes and four Ø2 mm passages through the copied carrier. Source pins remain nominal Ø1 mm; a separate Ø1.5 mm screen leaves 0.25 mm radial passage clearance. This is not an approved pin tolerance, solder land, insulation system or retained carrier. Source installation reservations remain 10 mm around/above and 5 mm below maximum pin projection, plus the existing film allocation. Electrical lead crossings of that reservation are expressly unqualified construction interfaces.

## Actual S12 geometry and mechanical changes

`electrical-frozen/` contains the two unchanged S12 boards and the actual exported netlist. `actual-boards.json` is extracted with KiCad from those frozen originals: 45 rotating and 12 stationary footprints, 189 numbered pads. It retains actual pad identifiers, shared-pad features, drill/land dimensions, layers, nets, values and footprint IDs. Component solids are S12's saved **body/height allocations**, not invented supplier 3D profiles. The native document contains 360 single-solid CAD objects, including named reserves, alternate ribbon poses and individual lands; this is not a count of manufactured parts. The native assembly includes each physical land; the assembly STEP omits copper detail, supplied separately by the native model and land-detail export.

## Finite stationary and moving connections

The stationary board receives the module leads at its **actual J0 lands**, not at a disconnected flex-tail box. Each source lead has a modeled copper-equivalent Ø1 mm conductor, Ø2 mm insulation envelope, finite curves/turns and a bare terminal region. A retained two-piece guide beyond PCB edge Z8 captures the insulated straight sections; its upper half is integral with the existing axial-screw corner cap. Nominal channel contact is not a qualified pull-strength or insulation-compression specification. The last local correction smooths the terminal turns: minimum proposed centreline radii are 2.347 mm positive and 1.979 mm return. The earlier 0.629 mm positive turn was rejected because it was tighter than the allocated insulation radius. `wire-radius-check.json` checks 1,342 replacement-wire pairs, confirms the other 356 native parts unchanged within the documented save tolerance, and preserves the certified R24 motion bound. Both source-pin endpoints have explicitly bounded copper overlaps below 0.089 mm³; these are proposed solder junctions, not qualified terminations. These remain tight **engineering static bends**, not supplier-approved wire limits; a selected wire may require more space. Wire family, insulation OD/tolerance, current/temperature, source-pin soldering and unprotected module-to-U1 segment remain construction gates.

The fixed static flex extension is a ruled, developable20 mm-wide strip with two8 mm copper lanes and1 mm separation. It enters a bounded copied core/spool passage and terminates on the actual J2 pads. A removable bridge shoe captures the straight section, supported by the core; axial M1 screws avoid the active winding and PCB. A second developed strip connects the original moving tail to actual J3. Its removable shoe has outboard screw ears and dedicated driver passages outside the ribbon. At the moving terminal, the saddle ends at Y26.24, the 0.16 mm ribbon occupies Y26.24..26.40, and the shoe starts at Y26.40. Local shoe pockets and head/shaft passages remove the previously found support overlaps; screw pilot engagement is 0.74 mm. Neither static extension is counted as part of the1046.167655992167 mm active winding.

The proposed single-layer stack is70 µm rolled-annealed copper within0.16 mm total thickness, copper offsets−0.055..+0.015 mm. The two dielectric faces are25/65 µm allocations. Both faces of the **static** solder windows are open; there are no holes or plated features in the active bend. Explicit0.13 mm solder preforms connect exposed copper to the nominal35 µm PCB lands. The fixed end uses opposite-side access to the same copper layer, preserving positive/return lane order without crossover. Minco's retrieved guide lists one-layer/two-sided-access construction (p10); it does not approve this stack, forming, solder process or duty cycle. Its bend-radius discussion describes10× thickness as an indicator, not a qualification. `fanout-probe.json` and `moving-path.json` retain the actual proposed paths; the supplier must approve their static shapes, tolerances and retention. No passive elastic equilibrium or endurance is inferred from CAD.

The contact graph distinguishes physical copper/solder contacts from the **schematic-only, unrouted** path through U1 between J0 and J2. Thus this is not an electrically continuous finished source assembly. FPC positive is J2.1→fixed lane1→active lane1→moving lane1→J3.1; return is the corresponding lane2/GND_ISO. No mains or PE rotates.

## Assembly and access basis

1. Keep both stationary ends open. Install the copied spool/core interfaces and feed the20 mm static flex terminal through its bounded passage before winding/closing the rotor. The enlarged original static tail and stiffeners stay outside; no lead passes through solid adhesive.
2. Slide the stationary PCB axially into its edge seats. Solder J0 and J2 using the defined axial tool approach. The bridge retainer can be translated with2 mm radial lift inside the bore before lowering to its nominal seat; this sequence still needs physical assembly/fixture confirmation. Fit the axial M1 cap screws from the open core ends. Sourcepod/endcap attachment must not precede this access.
3. Slide the rotating PCB from +X onto its rails, with rear gate and loose fanout shoe absent. Fit the gate and two external M1 screws. Attach J3 using the axial solder-tool passage, then capture the static tail. Dedicated radial driver holes reach the two clamp screws outside the ribbon edge. These are engineering tool envelopes, not a guarantee for every driver/iron.
4. Close the rotor around the unchanged active winding and fixed spool, retaining the original frame-screw, pin, spring and cap assembly sequence. Verify actual return, all13 engagements and independent stops physically before any use.

No foam covers component tops and no conductive heatsink contacts unspecified pads. Thermal airspace is only geometric allocation; no sealed-collar heat path or temperature qualification follows.

Standalone FXX79–82.5 and TRUE1 77–80 mm lengths are not installed cable projections. Panel shoulders are not mating planes. Latch/hand sweeps, terminal/sleeve/tool profiles, model revisions and approved NEMA cords remain unknown; no guessed mated parts were added. Classic gray/B needs the specified B-to-A jumper to a fixture A inlet, never direct B-to-A mating.

The finite12×6.5×12 plug,18 mm relief andØ6×80 cable are engineering screens only. Exact USB plug body/mating datum and cable bend qualification remain open, as does every fixture fit. In particular, the wider NEMA flange may obstruct the USB cable screen; the Ø48 blank cannot establish clearance for it.

## Reproduction and acceptance

All writes stay here. The `before-*.FCStd` files are frozen proof snapshots, not alternative current assemblies. `check-local.py` intentionally rechecks the pre-seat snapshot; `check-repaired.py` composes the subtractive-seat proof against the pre-wire snapshot, and `check-wire-radius.py` then checks the final four replacement wires against the current native assembly. Development logs can contain superseded failed trials; only the named JSON checks and receipt constitute final acceptance evidence. Run `extract.py` with existing Flatpak KiCad10.0.7; run CAD scripts through `run.py` with `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen` and `ALLOCATION_SCRIPT=<script>`. `build.py` reproduces the native model from frozen sources. The final seat repair is purely subtractive: two rear driver counterbores, a wire-guide seat, moving-shoe/head/shaft passages, an end-gate face trim and full-depth pilot passages. `check-repaired.py` proves both changed solids are subsets of the pre-repair parts; every other cleaned BRep retains its topology and numeric tokens within 1e-12 save-normalization tolerance. This preserves prior zero intersections; all previous positive pairs involving those two supports are recomputed. Independent checks cover actual pad centroid/area/net contracts, full physical overlap records, source reservations, contacts, motion/stops/locks, tool and insertion paths, saved-solid validity and STEP reopen. `receipt.json` is the authoritative result: **a native-valid solid alone is not a geometry PASS**.

Unmet regardless of geometric outcome: retained/insulated source carrier and mains construction, variant endcaps/mates, S12 routing/protection/startup/thermal/current/fault behavior, supplier package profiles, flex stack/forming/endurance, wire/termination process, material/fastener/bond allowables, assembly and physical tolerances, EMC/USB/product safety certification, and compactness/fixture acceptance.
