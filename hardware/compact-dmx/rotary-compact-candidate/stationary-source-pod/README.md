## Evidence and assembly access

`stationary-source-pod.FCStd` and `build.py` are editable. `pod-shell-module-allocation.step` contains the nine housing/film/module/carrier solids; `complete-allocation.step` adds the labeled reservations and comparison geometry; `reservations.step` separates the planning volumes. Nested keepouts are not structural material and must not be interpreted as fabricated parts.

`check.py` reopens the native and allSTEP outputs, verifies one connected closed solid per object, source spacing, all36 physical-allocation pairs and239 directed spacing checks. It checks each external trunk/joint/branch/support/secondary reservation against both the insulation box and its tolerance expansion and against the housing. The three inflated trunk sweeps are mutually checked. This is not a blanket all-reservation non-overlap claim: nested body/insulation/tolerance volumes and branch-junction allocations are intentionally different categories.

The181-angle USB check uses the **saved frozen body and plug-screen solids**, plus the explicitly engineered relief/cable screens, against all six housing pieces:4344 comparisons.13 detent angles are retained; the conservative full collar envelope and actual fixed core bore separately check stationary allocation separation. Trunk containment is checked against the **actual circular bore**, not the overly conservative diagonal of a circular wire's bounding rectangle.

With the lid removed, an83×54×45 service/lift reservation startsZ29 above the carrier. It is checked against the remaining physical allocation. It does not approve energized servicing, fastener/tool access or a lift procedure for an as-yet unsupported carrier. Endcap assembly/strain relief and final support attachment need real part drawings before those operations can be qualified.

`allocation-sections.pdf` and its twoSVG pages have explicit equal projection scales and dimension labels. Both PDF pages were rendered with existingpdftoppm and visually inspected.
