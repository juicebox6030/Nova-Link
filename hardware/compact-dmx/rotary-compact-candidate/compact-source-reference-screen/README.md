## Safety, provenance and next proof

Both references separate primary/secondary domains; neither supplies this product's continuous PE trunk, touch-access enclosure, qualified fuse/terminal construction or mounting system. PMP F1 is1 A250 V0443001.DR; TIDA F1 is3.15 A250 VAC/VDC39213150000. Their ratings alone do not establish interrupt coordination, surge survival, inrush or safe inline-branch construction. Numeric installed PCB creepage/clearance, pollution/altitude/material conditions, enclosure spacing and full assembly insulation test coverage remain UNKNOWN. No invented reinforced whole-product rating.

A real TIDA-derived design would first need exact board/transformer revision and all maximum component profiles; a documented primary/secondary safety/layout and retained support construction; source current/derating/startup/fault budget including overload and winding utilization; transformer availability; EMI/thermal/insulation evaluation; and whole source/collar/connector packaging. It must resolve S12 output OVP energy/startup/current gates, not inherit a claim of compliance. No automatic redesign is authorized by this screen.

TI current [resource notice](https://www.ti.com/legal/important-notice-and-disclaimer.html) and bundled archive notice were read. These are not unrestricted open hardware. Retrieved assets remain local evidence; no vendor implementation was copied into product circuits or published. Supplier drawing rights are not broadened by retrieval.
