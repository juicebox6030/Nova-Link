One mechanical prototype in the unchanged Ø48 OD / Ø43 ID, 142 mm stationary core. No new power architecture, moving mains/PE, core hole, source rating or production safety claim. The original lead/trunk collision is addressed by the captured lift, not by moving the trunk. The native assembly and STEP include the exact saved baseline core, feedthrough, endcap blanks and trunk, with only the old display Y+180 translation removed. Frozen AXIAL-R1 and all sibling candidates remain read-only.

This is a conditional geometric integration. Primary branch wiring, fusing, connectors, released solder/PTH pattern, qualified insulation/retention, assembly motion, temperature and endcap safety remain **UNMET**. The broad source drawing bounds prevent approving the illustrated solder termination without a manufacturing drawing/process. The assembly is useful for integration review, not mains construction.

## Assembly sequence and precise limitations

1. Fit the floor film, board, module and adjustable jaws on the sled outside the core. Inspect the selected termination process; set lead projection and jaw height. Connect primary branch only under the later approved mains harness design.
2. Insert the sled through an open end with the secondary wires unformed or disconnected. The verifier deliberately demonstrates that a fully preformed loop translated axially strikes the intact core. Do not depict the finished loop as a slide-in assembly.
3. Feed the two secondary free ends through the existing Ø4 hole, form the R9 stationary path using a removable end-access jig, install both bellmouth halves from outside, and finish the split underside strain clamp. Joining at the pigtail may precede forming only if the joint/support survives the forming loads. Actual hand/tool access, jig extraction, motions, soldering sequence and springback are **UNMET**; no assembly motion simulation is claimed.
4. Inspect sleeve seating, cut/abrasion protection and clearances, verify dielectric isolation/PE continuity under the later safety plan, pull-test the retention and harness, and close the parent-owned endcaps. No additional drilling through the core into wires is proposed.

The carrier/module envelope is an axial packing proposal; real tolerances of the core bore/ovality, endcap fasteners and variant primary harness must be supplied before fit acceptance. The native saved solids verify only the specified nominal core and the enumerated allocations. Tool-access dimensions and field assembly are not inferred from solid nonintersection.

The cached [MEAN WELL PCB installation manual](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/IRM-01/PCB.pdf), installation items2–5, explicitly lists IRM and calls for bottom insulation distance/film, surrounding space, orientation and ventilation. Its generic **5 mm bottom /10 mm surrounding** guidance is not met or qualified by this tight assembly. A literal5 mm module-to-PCB standoff is incompatible with2.5 mm minimum pins, so applicability to this potted PCB module and the intended external insulation surfaces needs an authoritative installation/safety interpretation; it is not silently waived. The module's source ClassII/noFG and component approvals do not certify the assembled barrel. No PE-to-secondary/shell bond is added here. L/N/PE remain stationary in their reserved trunk; parent owns the shell/PE policy, variant endcaps and full mains safety design.

Free-air source derating curves do not establish sealed-barrel temperature or cooling. The small remaining voids, compression shoes, film and module orientation are not airflow. Full-load/line/ambient derating, touch temperature, hot-case clearances, wire/solder/insulator thermal ageing and fire containment remain **UNMET**. No extra size, airflow or mains rating is granted.
